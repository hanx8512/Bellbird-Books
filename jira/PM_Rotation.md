# Project manager rotation

A3 requires each team member to take a turn as project manager. Use this
rotation in the sprint planning record and Jira assignee history.

| Sprint | Project manager | Main focus | Handover evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sprint 1 | 杨世垣 | Catalogue, section inventory, Jira setup | Sprint 1 planning and review notes |
| Sprint 2 | 刘汉 | Special orders, reservations, supplier import | Sprint 2 board and review notes |
| Sprint 3 | 李宁杰 | Tests, release, clean checkout, handover | v0.2.0 release and retrospective |

The PM role covers facilitation, board hygiene, blocker escalation and sprint
goal reporting. It does not remove the delivery responsibility of the other
members.
